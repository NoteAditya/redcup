RedCup in one line
RedCup is India's pay-for-results creator marketing platform: brands post campaigns,
eligible creators apply, RedCup shortlists the best fit, the brand picks who to award, and
creators earn a guaranteed base per approved reel plus pay for every verified view.

Pitch: Meta Ads, but with real creators — brands pay a small base, then only for verified
views.

What makes it different: results over followers, numbers from Instagram's own data,
money held upfront, public results boards, and rankings that can't be bought.


The model
Every campaign is a bounty: a reel brief the brand funds upfront. Eligible creators apply,
RedCup shortlists the best fit, and the brand awards it. Creators are paid a base for
approved, live work and a performance amount for verified views.

How a campaign runs (about 2–3 weeks)

 1. Brand posts the campaign: brief, eligibility criteria (followers, niche, city, language),
    base per creator tier, rate per 1,000 views, budget and number of creators — and funds
    it through the payment partner.
 2. Eligible creators apply: creators who meet the criteria get a WhatsApp alert and apply.

 3. RedCup shortlists: we filter applicants by niche, past results and fit for the campaign,
    and send the brand a shortlist.
 4. Brand selects and awards: the brand picks its creators; each awarded creator's
     maximum earning is reserved from the budget.
 5. Creative direction and draft: awarded creators get the brand's direction, make the
    reel and send a draft; the brand approves it or asks for one change.
 6. Creator posts the approved reel with the Paid Partnership label.
 7. Tracking and payout: RedCup tracks verified views for 7 days, checks for fraud, and
     pays by UPI.
 8. Results: a public results board ranks every awarded creator; unused budget goes back
    to the brand.

How creators are paid

     Base: paid once the approved reel has been live 7 days. It starts from the creator's
     follower tier and, after 3 bounties, is set by their own results.
     Performance: a rate per 1,000 verified views, set by the brand.
     Cap: base + performance can't exceed the per-creator maximum. Fake views cancel
     both.

Business model
RedCup earns on both sides of every bounty — about 22% of the money that flows
through it — and adds service fees on top. It stays a platform: services must never
become most of the revenue.

Expansion plan
RedCup grows in four phases, and each one starts only when the last one proves itself —
the first gate is 40% of brands coming back by mid-January 2027.

expansion roadmap · 2026 to 2028

How each phase expands

     Launch: win D2C brands across India, starting with categories where reels sell (beauty,
     food and beverage, fashion); prove creators deliver and brands return.
     Prove and automate: turn everything done by hand into software — alerts, results
     boards, the RedCup Score, fraud checks — then raise a seed round on real repeat-
     brand numbers.
     Scale: bring in agencies as a sales channel, add YouTube Shorts, and sell content rights
     so the best reels become paid ads.
     The ad network: brands set a goal and budget and RedCup's AI runs the campaign;
     pay-per-customer for local businesses through creator QR codes and coupons; the
     RedCup Score becomes a data product and the base for creator financing.
